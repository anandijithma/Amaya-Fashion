Our Story

Founded in 2023 with a passion for quality, style, and performance, Amaya Fashion is dedicated to creating premium athletic and lifestyle wear that empowers individuals to look and feel their best.

  • Founded: 2023
  • Headquarters: Colombo, Sri Lanka
  • Collections: Women & Men
  • Premium Quality: Guaranteed
  • Products: 120+ Styles
  • Customers: 5000+
  • Sustainability: Focused
  • Warranty: Full Coverage

At Amaya Fashion, we believe that quality athletic wear should be accessible to everyone. Our curated collection features stylish, comfortable, and durable pieces designed for both active lifestyles and everyday wear. From performance-driven activewear to versatile casual pieces, we're committed to delivering excellence in every stitch.
